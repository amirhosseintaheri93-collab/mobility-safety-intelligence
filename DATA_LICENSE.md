## Materials not included in the public release

The complete simulation event tables and large generated hotspot HTML files are intentionally excluded pending a separate data-release decision. The public demonstrator is a deterministic, stratified sample and must not be represented as the complete research dataset.

## Third-party material

OpenStreetMap-derived street context remains subject to the Open Database License and its attribution requirements; it is not relicensed under CC BY 4.0. See `NOTICE.md`.

`llm/literature_benchmark.json` contains attributed benchmark values and paraphrased methodological notes from Taheri et al. (2026), DOI `10.1186/s12544-026-00774-9`. That article and its supplementary material are available under CC BY 4.0. The JSON identifies the source, links the licence, and labels any reconstructed display curve.
